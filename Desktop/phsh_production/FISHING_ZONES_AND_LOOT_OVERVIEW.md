### **Main Regions:**
1. **Big Island Waters** - Central starting area (radius: 150)
2. **X Isle** - Northern island region

4. **Mud Isle** - Eastern island

6. **Shadow Deep** - Deep water south of Shadow Isle
7. **Rock Deep** - Extreme southeast deep water
8. **Tree Isle** - Southern island

10. **Pier Deep** - Deep water far west
11. **Toolmaster's Island** - Southwest crafting-focused island
12. **Toolmaster's Dock Deep** - Deep water near Toolmaster Island

### **Subzones on Big Island:**
- **Spawn Pier** - High traffic area near spawn

### **Shadow Isle Subzones:**

### **Toolmaster Island Ponds:**
- **Toolmaster's Pond Lower 1 & 2** - Freshwater ponds
- **Toolmaster's Upper Pond** - Upper level pond

## 🎣 Fallback Loot Tables Overview

Fallback loot is what you get when you don't catch a fish. Each zone has its own fallback table with different drop rates.

### **Key Fallback Loot Items:**

#### **Common Items:**
- **Trash** - Discardable junk (5-35% chance depending on zone)
- **Old Can** - Low value kept item (10-45% chance)
- **Driftwood** - Loot material (8-40% chance, replaces seaweed)

#### **Bait Items:**
- **Shrimp** - Common bait (4-28% chance)
- **Fish Head** - Common bait (1-15% chance)
- **Worm** - Common bait, mainly in ponds (12-50% chance)
- **Glass Shrimp** - Rare bait (0.08-2.5% chance)
- **Squid** - Rare bait (6-20% chance in deep areas)
- **Glow Worm** - Rare bait, Shadow Isle specialty (8-20% chance)
- **Ghost Shrimp** - Rare bait, NIGHT ONLY on Shadow Isle (3-12% chance)
- **Rare Algae** - Rare bait, **OUTER ISLANDS ONLY** (10-12% chance)
- **Magnet** - Rare treasure bait (1.5-4% chance)

#### **Catchable Items (Like Chests):**
- **Runic Ore** - Epic catchable item that contains runes when opened (caught like fish/chests)
- **Treasure Chest** - Basic chest (5-12% chance)
- **Rare Chest** - Valuable chest (1-5% chance)
- **Legendary Chest** - Extremely rare (0.5-1% chance, deep water only)

### **Zone-Specific Fallback Highlights:**

#### **Best Zones for Rare Algae:**
- **Shadow Isle** - 10% chance
- **X Isle** - 10% chance
- **Tree Isle** - 10% chance
- **Rock Deep** - 10% chance
- **Cliffs Deep** - 12% chance (highest)
- **Toolmaster Island** - 10% chance

**Note:** Rare Algae is **NOT available on Big Island** - only on outer islands!

#### **Best Zones for Chests:**
- **Toolmaster Dock Deep** - 12% common, 5% rare, 1% legendary
- **Deep Coast** - 12% common, 5% rare, 1% legendary
- **Cliffs Deep** - 5% common, 2% rare, 0.5% legendary

#### **Best Zones for Ghost Shrimp (Night Only):**
- **Shadow Deep** - 12% chance
- **Shadow Isle** - 8% chance
- **Shady Beach** - 3% chance

## 💎 Runic Ore - Complete Guide

### **What is Runic Ore?**
Runic Ore is a **catchable epic item** (like chests) that can be fished up from the water. When opened, it contains valuable materials including runes used for rod enchanting.

### **Where to Catch Runic Ore:**

#### **Best Locations (Spawn Multipliers):**

1. **Rock Deep** - **1.0x multiplier** ⭐ VERY GOOD
   - Location: (238, 12, -73), radius: 25
   - Deep water area with excellent spawn rates

2. **Shadow Isle** - **1.2x multiplier** ⭐ EXCELLENT
   - Location: (179.5, 12, 154), radius: 75
   - Large area with high spawn rates

3. **Cliffs Deep** - **1.2x multiplier** ⭐ EXCELLENT
   - Location: (28, 12, -193.5), radius: 109
   - Very large deep water area

4. **Shadow Deep** - **0.8x multiplier** (Good)
   - Location: (194.5, 12, -0.5), radius: 55

5. **Pier Deep** - **0.6x multiplier** (Moderate)
   - Location: (-211.5, 12, 9), radius: 70

6. **X Deep** - **0.5x multiplier** (Moderate)
   - Location: (-16.365, 12, 174.07), radius: 55

**Subzone Bonuses:**
- **Deep Water** - **2.5x boost** (Major - best locations)
- **Deep Coast** - **2.0x boost** (Strong)
- **Kelp Bed** - **0.4x** (Reduced)

**Base Spawn Chance:** 0.15 (15% base chance, epic rarity)

### **What Runic Ore Contains When Opened:**

When you open a Runic Ore, you get one of these items (checked in order of rarity):

1. **Ark Rune** - **15% chance** ⭐ MOST RARE
   - Legendary rarity
   - Value: 500 coins
   - Only found in runic ores (cannot be purchased)

2. **Rune** - **35% chance**
   - Epic rarity
   - Value: 100 coins
   - Used for rod enchanting
   - Can also be purchased from Wizard (level 15+)

3. **Mithril Ingot** - **50% chance** (1-2 ingots)
   - Rare rarity
   - Value: 150 coins each
   - Essential for advanced rod crafting

4. **Mithril Nugget** - **80% chance** (3-6 nuggets, fallback)
   - Uncommon rarity
   - Can be crafted into mithril ingots

### **Runic Ore Stats:**
- **Rarity:** Epic
- **Base Value:** 200 coins (before opening)
- **Weight:** 250-300 (requires rod with 250lb+ capacity)
- **Base Chance:** 0.15 (15% base spawn chance)
- **Type:** Catchable loot item (like chests)
- **Spawn Times:** Any time (no time restrictions)

### **What Runes Are Used For:**

#### **Rod Enchanting:**
- **Regular Rune** - Used to enchant fishing rods with powerful abilities
- **Ark Rune** - Rarer, more powerful version (only from runic ores)
- **Wizard Dialogue:** "Runes enchant your fishing rod with powerful abilities. Regular runes can be purchased from me or found in runic ores. Ark runes are rarer and only found in runic ores."

#### **Requirements:**
- Level 15+ to purchase runes from Wizard
- Level 15+ to use runes for enchanting
- Must have an equipped rod that hasn't been enchanted yet

### **Farming Strategy:**
1. **Best Locations:** Shadow Isle (1.2x) or Cliffs Deep (1.2x) in deep water subzones (2.5x boost)
2. **Recommended Rod:** Any rod with 250lb+ capacity (Iron Rod or better)
3. **Recommended Bait:** Magnet bait (5x boost for runic ore) - also attracts chests!
4. **Tip:** Fish in deep water zones during hotspots for better overall loot
5. **Note:** Runic Ore is catchable like fish/chests, not from fallback loot tables

## 🌿 Rare Algae - Complete Guide

### **What is Rare Algae?**
Rare Algae is a **rare freshwater bait** with exceptional nutrients. It's superior to insects for freshwater fishing.

### **Where to Get Rare Algae:**

#### **Fishing Fallback Loot (Only Source):**
Rare Algae can **ONLY** be found on **outer islands** - it's **NOT available on Big Island**!

**Best Locations (10-12% chance):**

1. **Cliffs Deep** - **12% chance** ⭐ HIGHEST
   - Location: (28, 12, -193.5), radius: 109
   - Also has runebit (1.5%) and legendary chests

2. **Shadow Isle** - **10% chance**
   - Location: (179.5, 12, 154), radius: 75
   - Also has glow worms and ghost shrimp

3. **X Isle** - **10% chance**
   - Location: (-100, 12, 151.71), radius: 50

4. **Tree Isle** - **10% chance**
   - Location: (213, 12, -132), radius: 31

5. **Rock Deep** - **10% chance**
   - Location: (238, 12, -73), radius: 25

6. **Toolmaster Island** - **10% chance**
   - Location: (-171, 12, -190), radius: 74

**Important:** Rare Algae is **completely removed** from:
- Big Island fallback tables
- Default fallback table
- All Big Island subzones

### **What Rare Algae is Used For:**

#### **1. Freshwater Fishing Bait:**
Rare Algae is a **superior freshwater bait** with these stats:

- **Base Luck:** 1.1 (better than insect's 0.9)
- **Species Luck:** 2.2 (22% better effectiveness than insect's 1.8)
- **Catch Weight:** 1.1 (slightly better)
- **Catch Speed:** 1.05 (slightly faster)
- **Catch Zone:** 1.1 (slightly larger)

#### **2. Target Species:**
Rare Algae is effective for these freshwater fish:
- Goldfish
- Trout
- Largemouth Bass
- Koi Fish
- Marbled Koi
- Green Frog
- Poison Dart Frog

#### **3. Quest Item:**
- **Farmer Quest:** "Feed the Pigs" requires 5 rare algae
- **Reward:** Fly Rod
- **Quest Tip:** "Rare algae can be found while fishing - it's quite rare though, so keep casting!"

### **Rare Algae Stats:**
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Value:** 20 coins
- **Class:** Freshwater bait
- **Harvest Chance:** 0 (cannot be harvested, only found as loot)
- **Description:** "A rare freshwater algae with exceptional nutrients. Superior to insects for freshwater fishing."

### **Farming Strategy:**
1. **Best Location:** Cliffs Deep (12% chance) or any outer island (10% chance)
2. **Recommended Rod:** Any rod (no weight restrictions)
3. **Tip:** Fish in outer island zones - Big Island won't drop rare algae
4. **Use Case:** Best for freshwater fishing in ponds (Spawn Pond, Toolmaster Ponds, Central Fountain)
